How this works: each simulated year draws a return from a bell curve centred on your expected return. Expected return and volatility slide from your working-years values to your retirement values over the de-risking window, which is the glide path. Spending is withdrawn at the start of each retirement year. Real markets have fatter tails and runs of bad years, so read the success rate as a guide, not a promise.
